Bio

Welcome! I am a Computer Science PhD student at the University of Maryland, College Park, and a Research Intern at the Amazon Frontier AI & Robotics Team.

My research focuses on dexterous manipulation and interactive autonomy. I am especially interested in how robots can learn, plan, and adapt through physical interaction with the real world.

At the University of Maryland, I am a member of the Perception and Robotics Group, advised by Prof. Yiannis Aloimonos and Dr. Cornelia Fermüller. At the Amazon Frontier AI & Robotics Team, I work with Prof. Ruoshi Liu, Prof. Haozhi Qi, Dr. Akash Sharma Prof. Jiayuan Mao, as part of DexHand team led by Prof. Jitendra Malik . I also collaborate with Dr. Ji Zhang at Carnegie Mellon University. Previously, I was a research intern at Honda Research Institute USA and a visiting student in Prof. Fei Gao's FAST Lab at Zhejiang University.

I open source much of my research. Please visit my Github or email me if you would like to discuss the work.
